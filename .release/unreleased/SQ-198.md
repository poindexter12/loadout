---
ref: SQ-198
title: Negative-control failure-kind check scopes negation to its clause
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

The negative-control check now applies a negation to its whole clause, not to a fixed 40-character window. A marker line such as "not TypeError, ImportError, a module resolution failure, or a collection error" is accepted. A negation stops at sentence punctuation, brackets, dashes, and connectives such as "but" and "because", so "not an assertion mismatch but a collection error" is still refused. Every mention on the line is checked, so a negated first mention can no longer hide a real one that comes later.
