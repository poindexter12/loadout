---
ref: SQ-144
title: Negative-control check no longer requires untouched neighbouring tests
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

The negative-control check now attributes a changed line to a test only when the line falls inside that test's own declaration span. A helper, comment, or blank line added between two tests, or a change at the edge of one test, no longer makes the untouched neighbouring test a required negative-control report.
