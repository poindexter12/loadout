---
ref: SQ-258
title: Grant executors a write lease on approved foreign repository worktrees
bump: minor
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

Tickets accept `externalWorktrees`, absolute roots of approved checkouts in other repositories, set by the orchestrator through `add` or `update`. While its claim is held, the executor may Write and Edit under those roots instead of being refused with "no write lease for this linked worktree" and copying files in through Bash. A grant can never point inside the project or reopen another checkout of it, the claim holder cannot grant one to itself, and the board never commits there. A refused write into another repository's checkout now says so and names the grant.
