---
ref: SQ-172
title: Verify timeout no longer orphans its test runner when the wrapper is killed
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Verify no longer orphans its test runner when the waiting wrapper is killed. The verify command runs in its own process group so a timeout can kill the whole tree, but that also hid the tree from a kill aimed at the wrapper (such as the harness's own Bash timeout). The verify script now carries a watchdog that terminates its group, SIGTERM then SIGKILL after a grace period, once the waiting process is gone.
