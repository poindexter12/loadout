ref: SQ-394
title: ready no longer advertises tickets dispatch refuses
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

`ready` and `dispatch` now agree on what is workable. A write ticket with no declared file scope, a ticket with two prior no-commit attempts, and a review whose candidate was permanently rejected (or whose binding no longer matches its source) used to be listed as ready and placed in a wave, and then refused on dispatch. They also named themselves in the `higherPriorityReady` warning on every lower-priority dispatch. These tickets are now left out of the ready set, its waves, and that warning. `ready` lists them separately under `notDispatchable` (CLI: "Not dispatchable") with dispatch's own refusal text, a stable code, and the next step: declare files, or file a fresh repair and review. All three read one predicate shared with `dispatch`. `next` (claimNext) is unchanged: on a routed board it hands out only direct inline claims, which never pass through dispatch. Transient refusals, such as a gateway that is not ready or a dispatch attempt still winding down, are unchanged.
