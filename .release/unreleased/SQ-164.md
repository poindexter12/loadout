---
ref: SQ-164
title: Classify host sleep before declaring an executor stalled or dead
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

Stall and death triage now checks for host sleep first. On macOS Sidequest reads `pmset -g log` (elsewhere it compares wall-clock and monotonic time) and reports liveness `host_sleep`, records a watchdog death during sleep as `failureShape: host_sleep`, and counts only awake time against claim backstops, so a sleeping executor keeps its claim and the orchestrator is told not to change model routes. Orchestration guidance now says long unattended runs need the host kept awake (`caffeinate -is` or AC power).
