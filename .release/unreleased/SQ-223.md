---
ref: SQ-223
title: Tell the executor when a live verify is amended; refuse a verify the shell cannot parse
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

When the orchestrator amends a dispatched ticket's verify, the executor's next briefing now names the amendment and the superseded command. A capture of the superseded command is refused with the current verify-capture wrapper invocation, so the executor can rerun without a re-dispatch, and the update reply hands the orchestrator that invocation to relay. `add` and `update` now refuse a verify the shell cannot parse, such as prose like `scripts/gate.sh passes (exit 0), ...`, an unterminated quote, or unbalanced parentheses, instead of accepting it and failing only when an executor runs it.
