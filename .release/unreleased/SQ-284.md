---
ref: SQ-284
title: Board calls that omit project follow the caller's claim to its board
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

An MCP board call that named a ticket `ref` but omitted `project` always resolved to the session cwd's board. An executor whose claim lived on another board then wrote its later comments and progress onto the cwd board's unrelated ticket of the same ref, silently. Such calls now follow the caller's live claim: when the caller holds a claim on that ref (matched by `by`, or by the dispatch session when `by` is omitted) on another board, the call resolves to that board. A call that is ambiguous across boards, such as the same caller holding the ref on two boards, or a session-only match while the cwd board has its own ticket under that ref, is refused with a message asking for `project`. Calls with an explicit `project`, and calls with no matching claim, behave as before; `claim` keeps its own binding.
