---
ref: SQ-354
title: A hand-landed close accepts the fragment that actually announced its fix, and steps aside once the delivery commit is released
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

`groomClose --deliveryCommit <sha>` is the route for closing work that reached the integration branch by hand. Its release-fragment gate assumed the closing ticket was the sole author of its own delivery: within the delivered range it accepted only `.release/unreleased/<closing ref>.md`. A ticket whose fix was delivered incidentally, inside a sibling ticket's commit, could not be closed at all. Both ways out were wrong. Writing a second fragment under the closing ref double-announces one change in the changelog, and forcing the status by hand discards the delivery provenance `--deliveryCommit` exists to record.

The gate now accepts a fragment filed under any ref present in the delivered range, because the sibling's fragment is the one that announced the change. It also steps aside entirely when the delivery commit is already published under a release tag: that commit's changelog entry is written and immutable, so there is nothing a fragment could add.

The gate is narrowed, not removed. An unreleased delivery commit still has to carry a fragment somewhere in its range, and the range it scans is still the whole submitted range rather than the delivery tip commit's own diff. Submit is untouched and keeps the own-ref requirement, where the closing ticket really is the author of its candidate.
