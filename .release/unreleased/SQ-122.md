title: Verification timeouts name the observed time, the cap, and how to raise it
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

When integrate, groomClose, or an assembled-wave gate stops a verify at the integration cap, the refusal now states how long the command ran, the effective `integrationVerifyTimeoutMs` cap and its maximum, and the `board_config` route to raise it. It no longer tells you to refresh and reverify the candidates. Merged-tree verification results now record `durationMs`, and `board_config` output includes `integrationVerifyTimeoutMaxMs`.
