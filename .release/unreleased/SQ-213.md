---
ref: SQ-213
title: Negative-control marker parser accepts a target or assertion containing ';'
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

The `[sidequest:negative-control]` marker parser treated `';'` as a hard field separator for the target and assertion fields, so a target or assertion naming a quoted test (e.g. `parses "a;b"`) broke the whole-line match and was rejected as malformed or missing evidence even though the marker reported a genuine non-zero failure. The field regex now matches the target and assertion as the remainder up to the LAST `"; assertion="` and the LAST `"; "` before the trailing `<command> failed=<n>`, which is the real field boundary for the documented single-line format, so a `';'` inside either field no longer truncates the match.
