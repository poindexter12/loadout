ref: SQ-297
title: Move boards between Sidequest homes
bump: minor
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

New `sidequest move-board <board-ref>... --to-home <dir> [--from-home <dir>] [--dry-run] [--json]` moves whole boards from one `SIDEQUEST_HOME` to another, so work and personal boards can be split by account. It carries tickets (comments, claim history, submissions, dispatch records), stories, links, categories, board config, notifications, `projects/<slug>/` files and named dispatch token files, rewriting paths that pointed at moved files. Shared routing profiles are copied, never taken. Ticket ids and refs are unchanged, so `refs/sidequest/*` pointers stay valid; existing worktrees stay where they are. The move refuses, naming the cause, on a live claim, a launched or bound dispatch, a held publish lock, or a destination collision. `--dry-run` lists exactly what would move without creating the destination. Source rows are deleted only after the destination commits and its per-table row counts verify.
