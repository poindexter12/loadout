---
ref: SQ-349
title: Executor briefings report their launch budget and say whose context warning is whose
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Every executor briefing now ends with a measured launch budget: briefing characters, estimated tokens, share of a 167K usable window, and the three largest sections. Above 10% it adds a warning telling the executor to record the measurement before releasing. A file-transported briefing reports the budget in its pointer. The briefing also explains that a session-level context monitor (a statusline-bridge hook such as gsd-context-monitor) reports the orchestrator's usage, because a subagent shares its parent's session id. Measured executors opened at 56K to 59K tokens while that hook told them 100% and to ask the user. A genuine context exhaustion now carries a `context exhaustion:` release-reason prefix.
