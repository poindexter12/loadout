title: One capacity model behind the full suite's phase budget and the capture wrapper's deadline

The full suite's phase budget and the verification wrapper's outer deadline now come from one module, `src/lib/test-phase-capacity.ts`. `scripts/test-full.mjs` and `src/lib/verify-capture.ts` each held their own copy of the capacity constants, the effective-worker formula, and the `SIDEQUEST_FULL_SUITE_PHASE_BUDGET_MS` parser, under a comment asking the two to stay in lockstep; copied constants are the one implementation that cannot hold that invariant, and they had already drifted. The wrapper's `/^\d+$/` override parser rejected `1e6`, `+2500000` and `2500000.0` while the suite accepted them, so those spellings widened the phase the suite enforces and left the gate at the narrower deadline. One parser now serves both.

The capacity baseline was recalibrated with the model: the expected eight-worker phase duration moves from 480000ms to 960000ms and the phase ceiling from 2400000ms to 3600000ms. The 480000ms figure was measured against a 1618-test suite that no longer exists; the suite is now ~2019 tests, and the old baseline computed an 859641ms budget that killed a healthy low-load run with zero test failures, which is why every full-suite run on this host had been passing `SIDEQUEST_FULL_SUITE_PHASE_BUDGET_MS` by hand. The new baseline predicts the measured 1131971ms low-load phase to within 1.3% and budgets 1719360ms for it, and the ceiling clears the slowest measured 1907973ms phase by 1.89x.

The agent-facing capture-budget contract in `skills/sidequest/references/invocation-contracts.md` moves with the code: it now names the new 3600000ms ceiling and describes the wrapper's deadline as the suite's own phase budget plus the setup allowance, rather than a flat cap that happened to match.
