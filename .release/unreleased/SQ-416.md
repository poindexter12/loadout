US-8 wave 1. A ticket whose executor asked the user a blocking question now parks in the new `awaiting-user` status with a `park` record (`mode`, `qids`, `parkedAt`, and on a detach the `detach` cause). The park starts held: a turn end after a sent blocking question keeps the claim and worktree, and the holder's next board call resumes it to `doing`. A held park ignores idle silence and detaches to released on the 24 h abandon backstop, owner SessionEnd, a turn end whose question was never sent, or `release({kind:"question"})`. A released park keeps its original `parkedAt` and returns to `todo` once the last open blocking question is answered (`unparkTicket`); a held park returns to `doing` with its claim intact. An awaiting-user ticket is never ready, refuses dispatch and foreign claims with `awaiting_user`, and cannot be entered by a manual status change. A claim handed back to `todo` withdraws the questions it asked. `pulse` adds `questions:{open, oldest}` and the park record, and `release`, `submit`, `update`, `list`, board-move, the GitHub audit (labelled `status:in-progress`) and the CLI all know the new status.
