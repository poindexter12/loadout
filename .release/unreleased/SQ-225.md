ref: SQ-225
title: Captured verify runs survive the 10-minute Bash ceiling
bump: minor
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

The `verify-capture` wrapper takes `--detach`. It starts the capture as a detached child in its own process group and session, so killing the Bash call at Claude Code's 600000ms ceiling no longer kills the verify command or loses its record, and the capture keeps its own load-derived budget. The wrapper then waits in the foreground for up to 540000ms (`--wait-ms` lowers or raises that, capped at 570000ms). A capture that finishes relays its own output and exit code. One still running prints `verify=running`, exits 75, and prints a `--resume <run directory>` command that waits again. A run whose process ended without writing an exit status reports `verify=lost` and exits 2. Executor briefings now hand out the wrapper with `--detach` and tell executors to rerun the printed `--resume` command in the foreground instead of improvising an until-loop or backgrounding the verifier.
