---
ref: SQ-275
title: Landed delivery is proven only by the remote's live advertisement, never by local refs
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

Recording a delivery against a remote-mode integration target (`groomClose --deliveryCommit`, `integrate --delivery-commit`, and equivalent external reset/manual/working-tree deliveries) now proves the commit landed by asking the remote itself: a fresh `ls-remote` of the target branch, then fetching that advertised tip by SHA and checking ancestry against it. Local `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>`, the checkout's HEAD, and other local branches are never consulted, so a commit that only looks landed locally is no longer accepted. If the remote cannot be reached, recording now refuses with `landed_remote_unavailable` naming the remote and branch, rather than silently falling back to stale or local evidence. A genuine working-tree delivery still records against the checkout, and its revision is now explicitly tagged `git-checkout:<branch>` rather than the upstream remote it did not land on. Verification of a recorded delivery also now runs in a detached scratch worktree at the delivery commit, never in your own checkout.
