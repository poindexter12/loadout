title: Ticket filing is gated on whether the work was actually asked for
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]

Standing authorization now governs the route, not the mandate. A usable project route still settles that wanted work beyond a small task goes to a ticket and a dispatched executor instead of inline, with no need to offer that choice or ask again. It no longer implies that something the orchestrator merely noticed is work you want done. Before filing, it has to name who asked for the work and what breaks if it is never done; when the only answer is that it observed the problem itself, it reports that in its reply and files nothing.

The guidance previously answered how work gets done in detail and never asked whether the work should exist, so six separate strings pushed toward filing with no counterweight: authorization to file and dispatch triggered by any multi-file change, unknown location or investigation, plus three clauses that turned discoveries and unrelated review findings into tickets automatically. An orchestrator investigating a problem well therefore produced tickets mechanically; one session filed eleven, ten of which were never dispatched and none of which were the thing that had been asked for. Review findings and unrelated discoveries are now reported to you with their evidence and filed only when you want them tracked, or when the contract or a proven regression blocks the active ship.

Nothing changes about how tickets you do ask for are routed, sharded, dispatched or verified.
