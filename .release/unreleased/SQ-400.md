---
ref: SQ-400
title: Abandoning a submission refuses when the remote cannot be reached
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

Abandoning a pending submission (`groomClose` with `abandonSubmission: true`, `groom-close --abandon-submission`) on a remote-mode board no longer falls back to the local `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>` when the remote cannot be contacted. That fallback could record a candidate the remote had since lost as an `already-landed` delivery and close the ticket `done`. The abandon path now refuses with `landed_remote_unavailable` and records nothing, matching delivery reconciliation. Abandonment with a reachable remote, and on local-mode boards, is unchanged.
