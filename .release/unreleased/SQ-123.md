---
ref: SQ-123
title: update({verify}) re-pins a pending submission's integration verify
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

Changing a ticket's verify with `update({ref, verify})` after the executor has submitted now re-pins the command that the next integrate or groomClose merged-tree verification runs, so a verify that turned out too slow can be narrowed without abandoning the submission. The change is recorded on the ticket with the old and new commands, the update response says which verify runs next, and timed-out integration verifications point at this path. Clearing a pending submission's verify is refused, and the refusal names the command that still runs.
