---
ref: SQ-179
title: "sidequest: a board-owned capture waiver replaces hand-edited refs when captures fail only from host load"
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

When a candidate's verify capture fails only from host load (a capture-budget timeout, or a port or process race while other suites run) and reruns keep failing, the orchestrator can now post `[sidequest:capture-waiver] capture=<id> signature=<status[:exit-N]> authority=<who>; <reason and evidence>` on the ticket, and the executor resubmits the same candidate. The waiver binds one recorded timeout or failed_suite capture of that candidate, pinned command, and dispatch attempt; its signature must name that capture's failure exactly; the claim holder and the submitter cannot author it. The submission records status skipped with the waiver (affectedGate verification_capture_required), and integration verification still runs the pinned command. The verification_capture_required refusal and the verify-capture wrapper output now name this path, with the concrete capture id and signature, instead of leaving hand `git update-ref` edits of refs/sidequest as the only way out.
