title: "SessionStart no longer waits on the worktree sweep: it hands the sweep to the detached worker and the result reports on the next start"
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---
The sweep's whole-board bookkeeping took longer than the 2500ms SessionStart wait on every start, so each start paid the full wait and printed the "exceeded its SessionStart budget" notice. The worker now also runs under a bounded SQLite lock budget instead of the server's 15s x 3. `SIDEQUEST_SWEEP_DEADLINE_MS` still opts into a wait.
