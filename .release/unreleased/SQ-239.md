---
ref: SQ-239
title: Gateway no longer wedges after the Mac wakes from sleep
bump: patch
plugins:
  - model-gateway
---

The gateway's health check no longer blocks every request while it reads ChatGPT sign-in status from the Keychain, which could take up to 15 seconds after a wake. Sign-in status is now read in the background and the last known answer is served meanwhile. Health probes open a fresh connection instead of reusing a pooled one that may have died during sleep, idle pooled connections are dropped after a sleep-sized gap, and the supervisor answers `/healthz` with a 503 after 5 seconds rather than hanging when its worker is stuck.
