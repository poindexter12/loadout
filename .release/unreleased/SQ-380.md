title: Context budget benchmark measures dispatch warnings separately from the spawn payload
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest

Test-only fix. SQ-320 made dispatch warnings ride the compact result whole, which pushed the benchmark's dispatch from 1154 to 1598 bytes and failed its 1320 cap on every full-suite run. The benchmark now keeps the 1320 cap on everything except `warnings`, asserts the warnings arrive whole and unmarked, and caps the full result at 1680 bytes.
