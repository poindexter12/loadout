ref: SQ-63
title: Name why the /v1/models probe failed instead of reporting a bare false
bump: patch
plugins: [model-gateway]
---

Guardian log lines, `ensure` startup failures and Codex readiness messages now name why the proxy's `/v1/models` probe failed: `no answer within 2000ms`, `ECONNREFUSED`, a reset while reading the response body, an answer on a reused keep-alive socket, or a non-200 status. Previously all of those collapsed into one indistinguishable "did not answer", which is why a probe failing every other check for eleven days could not be diagnosed. The three copies of the probe are now one shared implementation. Failure thresholds and restart behavior are unchanged.
