---
ref: SQ-167
title: A continuation that ends before any runtime binds it no longer strands its retained worktree
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

A released isolated attempt with retained work is re-dispatched as a continuation bound to that retained worktree. If that continuation expired before launch (prepared-dispatch TTL sweep), or launched and was then failed by session reconciliation without ever binding, every later dispatch refused: the checkout was neither a board WorktreeCreate to reclaim nor a released attempt to resume. The next dispatch now inherits the continuation the unbound attempt was prepared from (its release, base, recorded revision, and bound identity) and re-validates the retained worktree from scratch (registration, checkout identity, revision, and the dirty/committed-progress rules), then resumes it in place as `dirty_worktree_resume` or `retained_worktree_resume`.

When a retained checkout cannot be reclaimed and cannot resume, the refusal no longer blames an incomplete WorktreeCreate for a binding it did not create. It names the real binding source, the continuation's resume refusal reason, and what to do: restore the checkout to its release state, or remove it once its work is saved elsewhere. The prepared-dispatch expiry sweep also records the retained worktree's revision at expiry.
