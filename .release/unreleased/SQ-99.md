---
ref: SQ-99
title: Recorded delivery accepts a reviewed descendant of the pinned candidate
bump: patch
plugins:
  - sidequest
---

Recording delivery (`integrate` with `deliveryCommit`, or `groomClose` delivery) now accepts a reviewed fix committed on top of the pinned candidate. Name that commit as `deliveryInteractionCommit`: it must descend from the pinned candidate and change submitted paths only. The delivery is then checked against the reviewed commit's content instead of the candidate's. This covers the common case where the candidate and a review fix land together as one squash commit, and a `manual` or `working-tree` delivery whose working tree carries the fix. Before this, those deliveries were refused with `delivery_content_missing` and the only way to close the ticket was `abandonSubmission`, which records delivered work as abandoned. The record uses delivery kind `reviewed-candidate-descendant` and content evidence ending `:reviewed_candidate_descendant`. The `delivery_content_missing` refusal now explains that this route exists, and says so when the named commit does not descend from the candidate.
