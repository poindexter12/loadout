---
ref: SQ-322
title: "sidequest: close an externally delivered ticket after a later merge changes its files"
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

A ticket delivered as a forge pull request that was squash-merged could never be closed once a later merge changed one of its submitted files. Its pinned candidate is not reachable from the integration branch after a squash, so a manual delivery was checked only against today's integration working tree. After the later change, that check refused with `delivery_content_missing` on every attempt, and the only other way out recorded delivered work as abandoned.

A manual delivery is now proved at the point it landed. When the working tree no longer matches, the board looks back through the integration branch's first-parent history after the candidate's fork point for the oldest commit whose submitted files hold exactly the candidate's content, or its reviewed descendant's when `deliveryInteractionCommit` is given. It records that commit as the delivery's `landingCommit`. A fork point that already held that content proves nothing and never matches, and a delivery that no commit on the branch ever carried is still refused with the same message as before.
