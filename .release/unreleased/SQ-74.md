---
ref: SQ-74
title: In-test wait deadlines scale with host load, the way the capture budget around them does
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

The full-suite capture budget already widened when the host was busy with work it did not start, but the waits inside the suite stayed at a fixed deadline per test worker. A sibling suite that never took the capture slot could therefore starve a pinned verification into a false red. `verify-capture` now exports the one effective-worker model (`effectiveTestWorkers`) and `capacityScaledDeadlineMilliseconds`, and the in-test waits in `hooks.test.ts` and `verify-capture.test.ts` go through a shared helper that scales SQ-58's per-worker deadline by the same capacity factor. The helper re-reads the load on every poll: rising load extends a deadline, and falling load never shortens one. On an idle host the deadlines are unchanged. Ruling: an ad-hoc full-suite run still does not take the capture slot. The slot serialises recorded captures, and load from any source is now absorbed by the scaled deadlines instead.
