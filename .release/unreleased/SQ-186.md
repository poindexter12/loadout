---
ref: SQ-186
title: "sidequest: groomClose manual delivery checks the whole submitted range for a release fragment, not just the delivery tip commit"
bump: patch
plugins: [sidequest]
---

`groomClose`'s delivered-release-fragment check previously derived changed paths from `commitPaths(repo, delivery.commit)` — the delivery tip commit's own diff only. A multi-commit candidate whose fragment landed in an earlier commit (with a later, unrelated tip commit under the same shipped plugin path) was falsely refused with `missing_release_fragment` once main had already fast-forwarded to that tip commit, even though the fragment was present in the submitted range and on main. The check now prefers the already-recorded range-wide `changedPaths` from the reconciled delivery, or recomputes paths across the recorded submission base..delivery commit range (`commitScope.commitRangePaths`), falling back to the tip commit only when no base was ever recorded.
