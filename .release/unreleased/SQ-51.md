---
ref: SQ-51
title: model-gateway and observability tests no longer reserve ports by binding and releasing them
bump: patch
plugins: [model-gateway, observability]
---

Test-only change; no runtime behavior differs. Eight more model-gateway test files (`context-window`, `rc-compat-mode`, `session-route-cache`, `gateway-telemetry`, `tool-schema-sanitize`, `tool-search`, `grok-backend`, `gateway-upstream-errors`) and the observability observer test stop binding a port, closing it and handing the freed number to a spawned process, which let a concurrent test worker take the port first. Spawned gateways now start on `CODEX_GATEWAY_PORT=0` and report their real port on stdout, test-owned servers keep the port they bound, a deliberately dead upstream uses port 1, and the few ports that must be known before a spawn (the RC-compatibility listener and the observer handoff port) are process-scoped and below the OS ephemeral ranges. This finishes the sweep SQ-37 started.
