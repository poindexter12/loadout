---
ref: SQ-407
title: "groomClose closes tickets whose work landed by rebase, fold, or later edits instead of forcing a false abandonment"
bump: minor
plugins: [sidequest]
---

`groomClose` and hand delivery no longer insist that the pinned candidate commit itself is on the
target. Work that reached the target another way is recorded as delivered, with the evidence named:

- **`patch_equivalent`**: every candidate commit's patch (verbatim patch-id) is on the target's landed
  history since the merge base, as after a rebase or cherry-pick. The board finds this without being
  told, and refuses a landing that was later reverted.
- **`landed_diff_equivalent`**: the orchestrator names `landingCommits` and the candidate's diff
  reverse-applies on the commit that carried it but not on the parent of the oldest named commit, as
  after a fold into a larger commit or a landing that was later edited.
- **`attested`**: a new orchestrator-only `deliveryMethod: "attested"`, the last resort, over named
  `landingCommits` and a non-empty reason. The integration outcome is `attested` (with `attestedAt`),
  never `verified` and never `abandoned`.

Every route refuses landing commits that are not reachable from the target's live tip (on a
remote-mode board, a commit present only on this machine is refused, as in SQ-275). It also refuses
landing commits that touch none of the candidate's paths, and an attested delivery that names none.
Live-claim and open-dispatch refusals, SQ-186 fragment range coverage, and merged-tree verification at
the landing commit are unchanged.

When a candidate's paths have landed history, refusal messages now list those commits and offer the
equivalence and attested routes before `--abandon-submission`. The MCP `groomClose` tool gains a
`landingCommits` argument, and its `deliveryMethod` enum gains `attested`. The CLI does not expose
them yet.
